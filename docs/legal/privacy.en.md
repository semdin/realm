# Thousand Realms Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-11-19

This policy explains what Thousand Realms ("the game") stores about you, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. The game is run by its independent developer ("we"). We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, birth date or payment details. The game has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

## What we store

- **Account:** your username, a one-way hash of your password (Argon2id), a one-way hash of your recovery code, when the account was created, your last login, when you changed your password, and which version of the terms you accepted and when, including your confirmation that you are 16 or older. We cannot read your password or recovery code.
- **Characters:** name, house, level, appearance, items, gold, quests, skills, position and other progress, with creation and update times.
- **Dungeon boards:** the fastest runs of the week show character names and levels to other players.
- **Moderation:** bans and chat limits on your account with their reason, the players you have blocked, and a history of moderation actions.
- **Reports:** when a player reports someone, we keep the names of both, the category, what the reporter wrote, the map, and the recent chat lines around that moment.
- **On your device:** your settings, your language and, if you tick the box, your username. Your password is never saved there.

## Chat

Chat messages are sent to the players who should receive them. We do not write chat to our server logs, and we save it in our database only as part of a player report, as described above. The game servers hold recent local, shout and party lines in memory for up to 15 minutes so that a report can include them; after that they are gone, and a server restart clears them sooner. Remember that other players can read, copy or screenshot what you write.

## IP addresses and logs

Your IP address is needed to connect you to the game.

- The login service keeps it in memory to limit password guessing and mass sign-ups. These records last at most 24 hours and are not written to the database.
- Our web server may record an IP address when a request fails.
- Server logs record events such as logins and joins with numeric account and character numbers. They are kept for up to 30 days.

The launcher downloads the game and its updates from GitHub, and its own updates too unless the itch app manages it. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens.

If you get the launcher through itch.io, itch.io delivers that download and, in the itch app, the launcher's updates. itch.io then handles your IP address and any itch.io account you use under its own terms and privacy policy (https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy). You do not need an itch.io account to play. Your game account is separate and we do not link it to itch.io. itch.io may show us statistics about its page, such as views and downloads.

## Why we use this data

- To provide the game and your account (the agreement between us).
- To keep the game secure, stop cheating and abuse, and enforce the rules (our legitimate interest in a safe game).
- To meet legal duties, if any apply.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

Other players see your character name, house, level, appearance, equipment and chat. Our hosting provider runs the servers that hold the data. GitHub hosts the game's downloads and the public support page, and itch.io hosts the launcher where it is offered there. Data may be processed outside your country, including where these providers operate. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Account and characters: until you delete them, or until we close the account or the game.
- Login-protection records in memory: at most 24 hours.
- Recent chat in game server memory: at most 15 minutes.
- Reports: up to 180 days.
- Bans, chat limits and block lists: while your account exists, or until they are lifted.
- Server logs: up to 30 days.
- Database backups: replaced on a rolling basis, normally within 8 days.

## Deleting your data

You can do this yourself in the game:

- **Delete a character** on the character screen by typing its name.
- **Delete your account** in the Account settings with your password and by typing your username. All of its characters go with it.

You are disconnected first, and you leave any party, duel or dungeon as if you had logged out. Deletion cannot be undone. Your account row, password hash, recovery code hash and characters are removed. Your bans, chat limits and block list go with the account. Your character names are removed from the dungeon boards and from other players' block lists, though the run itself stays without your name. Reports that involve or mention you lose the names, the written text and the chat lines, and moderation history loses its details. Your username and character names can then be taken by someone else.

**What remains:** an anonymous record that an account or character was deleted, with the time and number of characters and nothing that links it to you; the category, map, time and status of reports, and the type and time of moderation actions, with nothing that identifies you; server logs until they expire; backups until they are replaced; and login-protection records until they expire. Anything you posted on GitHub is outside the game and is managed there.

## Your rights

Under the EU and UK GDPR you may ask for access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction, a portable copy, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Article 11) you may learn whether your data is processed, ask for information about it and its purpose, learn who it is shared with in Türkiye or abroad, ask for correction or deletion and for those changes to be passed on, object to a result that goes against you from automated analysis alone, and claim compensation for unlawful processing.

You can also complain to your data protection authority, which in Türkiye is the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

## How to reach us

Open an issue at https://github.com/semdin/realm/issues and give only your username and what you are asking for. The page is public, so never post your password, recovery code or other private information. We will ask you to prove from inside your account that it is yours before acting on a request.

## Children

The game is not for anyone under 16. If you believe a younger person has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the version date above changes and you will be asked to review it the next time you log in.
